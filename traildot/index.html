Privacy Policy

TrailDot

Last updated 10 September 2026

TrailDot collects no personal data whatsoever, and makes no network connections at all. Your location never leaves your device — the app has no networking code in it to send it anywhere.

Your location

TrailDot asks for your location while you are using the app, so it can draw where you are standing on the paper map you imported. It also reads the compass heading, to point the marker the way you are facing.

  • Your position is used to draw a dot on screen, and for nothing else.
  • It is not recorded: the app keeps no track log, no breadcrumb history, no list of places you have been.
  • It is not transmitted, because the app makes no network requests of any kind.
  • The app does not request background or "always" location. It stops reading your location when you leave the map view.

If you calibrate a map by standing at a landmark, the coordinates you capture are saved alongside that map on your device, because that is what makes the map line up. They stay on the device with the map.

The camera

TrailDot asks for the camera only so you can scan a paper map or a trailhead sign. It is used while the scanner is open and at no other time.

What the app stores, and where

Your imported maps and their calibration data are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no iCloud sync. Deleting the app deletes your maps and their calibrations.

Maps

TrailDot uses no third-party map or tile service. The maps are the ones you bring. Apple's Maps is used in one place only — an optional picker for choosing a landmark's coordinates during calibration — and is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.
